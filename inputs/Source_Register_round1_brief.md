## Hypotheses in the deck (not yet supported by evidence)
- Dispatchers lack a timely view of destination and en-route conditions when they decide (S2, S6).
- Destinations are chosen without sight of arrivals at other markets (S3).
- WRI's local failure points grow in size on long inter-state hauls (S3).
- India's ambient mandi-to-market setting is under-studied in routing research (S7, S9).

## What this deck does not contain
No interviews, field visits, surveys, simulations or experimental results of our own. Every stakeholder difficulty comes from published studies.
