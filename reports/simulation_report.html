6. What this does and does not show

  • Supply lots are SYNTHETIC (seeded); roads (OSM), weather (Open-Meteo replay) and daily market volumes (Agmarknet exports) are real.
  • Congestion and disruptions are modelled (ASSUMPTION A9), never observed traffic.
  • Decay uses cold-storage reference lives extrapolated with FAO's generic temperature curve (A2, A3, A17); the plausibility check above shows how far the absolute levels may be off. Comparisons between policies are more robust than absolute levels, but both depend on these assumptions.
  • Planned destinations follow the day's realised arrivals (A24), which gives the baseline implicit demand knowledge.
  • Dispatcher needs have not been validated by interviews. The system recommends; humans decide.
  • No percentage reduction is claimed or targeted. The deck's 14% figure is provisional and unsourced and is not used.
